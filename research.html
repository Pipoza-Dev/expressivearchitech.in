Subterranean Baoli Thermodynamics

Stepwells (Baolis) in Gujarat and Rajasthan remained up to 18°C cooler during 45°C summers by capturing cold underground bedrock radiation and retaining moisture in layered stone terraces. We simulate this in modern residential atriums using subterranean glass heat exchangers.
